ABOUT SHEDSHARE

Useful things, shared nearby.

ShedShare is a local rental marketplace where people can find useful items offered by nearby owners. It gives owners a place to share equipment and other belongings they are willing to rent, while helping renters find what they need for a project, event, trip, or short-term task.

Our aim is to make borrowing and renting easier to explore, with clear listings, direct conversations, and rental terms that both sides can review before they agree.

01

Find an item

Browse available listings by item and location. Open a listing to review its photos, condition, price, rental period, and pickup area. Ask the lister questions through ShedShare before making a decision.

02

Agree clearly

Before a rental is confirmed, both sides can review the dates, price, item condition, handover, and return expectations. The renter should check the agreement carefully and raise questions before accepting.

03

Share responsibly

Owners can list items with their own photos and an accurate description. Sharing the condition, included accessories, known defects, pickup arrangements, and rental rules helps renters know what to expect.

FOR RENTERS

Choose with care

Compare listings and contact the owner when you need clarification. Confirm the item, dates, price, and handover details in the app before proceeding with a booking.

FOR OWNERS

Set expectations

Use clear photos, set a fair rental price, keep availability up to date, and explain any conditions a renter should know. Respond to questions so both sides can make an informed choice.

YOUR PRIVACY

Pickup details stay private

Public listings show an approximate location to help people browse nearby. The exact pickup address is kept private and is shared with the renter only when the rental reaches the appropriate confirmed stage.

ShedShare helps people discover listings and manage rental conversations and booking steps. Payment and delivery options depend on the services available for that rental. A payment is confirmed only after the app verifies it; a screenshot or message is not proof of payment.

HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Here are the basics for browsing, listing, renting, and communicating on ShedShare.

How do I list an item?

Create an account, choose “List an item,” add accurate details and clear photos, disclose known defects, set your price and rental conditions, then submit the listing.

How do I contact a lister?

Sign in and open a listing to start a conversation. Keep rental questions and arrangements in ShedShare messaging.

When is a rental confirmed?

A rental is confirmed only after the app verifies the payment through its configured payment provider. A screenshot or message alone is not confirmation.

Can I arrange delivery?

Delivery options vary by listing and platform configuration. Check the rental details and agree on handover arrangements before accepting.

What should I include in a listing?

Use your own accurate photos and describe the item, its condition, what is included, any known defects, pickup area, price, and rental expectations.

How do I report a problem?

Use the report controls in a conversation where available, or contact the ShedShare support team through the support contact provided by the operator.

Can I delete my account?

Open Account & Settings in your member dashboard and choose “Delete my account.” Some transaction and safety records may be retained in anonymized form.

For a question not answered here, contact ShedShare support. This FAQ is general guidance; the terms shown for a particular rental still matter.
